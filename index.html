Jonathan Cazabonne

Mycologist, M.Sc. | Ph.D. candidate — Institut de recherche sur les forêts, Université du Québec en Abitibi-Témiscamingue (UQAT)

I seek to fill knowledge gaps in the diversity and ecology of fungi, and to help bring that knowledge into conservation frameworks and ecosystem-based forest management. We no longer have the luxury of time. Let’s fill the gaps and turn data into action. I’m deeply convinced that documenting and understanding the diversity of life in its environment — especially fungi — is essential if we want to protect it for the long term.
