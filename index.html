    Jonathan Cazabonne

    Mycologist, M.Sc. | Ph.D. candidate — Institut de recherche sur les forêts, Université du Québec en Abitibi-Témiscamingue (UQAT)

    I hold a B.Sc. in organismal, population and ecosystem biology and an M.Sc. in forest ecology and management, and I'm now a Ph.D. candidate at the Institut de recherche sur les forêts (UQAT), supervised by Maxence Martin and Mélanie Roy. My research aims for a finer understanding of fungal diversity — morphological and genetic — and of fungal interactions with other kingdoms, approached through molecular ecology, community ecology, integrative taxonomy, citizen science, and even literature and art.
